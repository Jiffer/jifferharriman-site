Ballet Boxes are two illuminated, sound-reactive platforms built to support
dancers. Each box glows from within and responds to sound in real time, so
the light shifts with the music and the room around it.

Commissioned by Boulder Ballet in 2024, they debuted at the company's AMPLIFY
Gala at the Dairy Arts Center in Boulder.
