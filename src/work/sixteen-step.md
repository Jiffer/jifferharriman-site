A multiplayer instrument you walk and jump on. Vibration sensors under each hexagonal
step detect footfall and fire a MIDI note, so musical patterns emerge out of how people
move through the space.

The steps are tuned to a pentatonic scale against drum sounds — rhythmic, improvisational,
and playable by anyone who wanders in.
