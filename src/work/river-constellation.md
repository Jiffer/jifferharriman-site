This is a fun multi-channel audio installation with these cool globe lights. It uses transducers to turn the steel poles into the speakers for a subtle sound experience that is ever evolving.

I developed the audio system for this permanent site-Responsive, multi-media public sculpture. Collaboration with Natalia Zubko and Beau Kenyon and Dispersion.
