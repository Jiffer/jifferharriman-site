Wanderers is a geo-spatial sound walk app built as a companion to the Colorado
Scale Model Solar System (CSMSS) — a series of plaques tracing the solar
system at a scale of 1 to 10 billion, running from the Fiske Planetarium out
along Colorado Avenue on the CU Boulder campus.

Designed by Teri Rueb (director), Roberto Azaretto (sound design and
composition), and myself (audio programming and app design), commissioned by
the Fiske Planetarium.

The app runs real-time interactive sound synthesis in Pure Data (Pd). Each
planet is sonified from its relative mass and orbital period into its own
soundprint, and the mix shifts as you move — orbit rate, hearing range, and
heading are all adjustable, and individual planets can be toggled on or off.
Sound is spatialized to your location and heading, so headphones are highly
recommended.

Live at the Fiske Planetarium since December 2021.
