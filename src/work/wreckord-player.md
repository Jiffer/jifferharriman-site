title: The Wreckord Player
year: 2016
tagline: A record player that forgets what it's playing.
kinds: [instrument, sound]
role: Concept, hardware, software
---

The Wreckord Player intercepts a record player's audio and runs it through a
granular synthesizer on a Raspberry Pi. The volume and tone knobs still turn —
they've just been reassigned to synthesis parameters, so the same gestures that
used to control a stereo now tear the sound apart.
