Ballet Boxes

2024 · installation / commission / light / sound

Two sound-reactive boxes that glow from within, built for dancers to stand on.

Ballet Boxes are two illuminated, sound-reactive platforms built to support dancers. Each box glows from within and responds to sound in real time, so the light shifts with the music and the room around it.

Commissioned by Boulder Ballet in 2024, they debuted at the company's AMPLIFY Gala at the Dairy Arts Center in Boulder.
