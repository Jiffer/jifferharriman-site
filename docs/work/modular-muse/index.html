Modular Muse

2016 · sound / research / toolkit

Toolkits so other people can build the instruments.

Developed as part of my PhD at the ATLAS Institute: two platforms for building musical robots and for mapping tangible interfaces to sound.

The musical robots platform is a set of MIDI-controlled motor driver boards and laser-cut mechanisms. Sound Clippys makes it quick to build a tangible interface and map it to sound — used in workshops and in a middle school music classroom where students designed and built their own instruments.

Role

Research, hardware, software, curriculum
