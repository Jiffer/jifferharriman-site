B.E.A.T.

2019 · public / collaboration

A recurring gathering for Boulder's artists, engineers, and everyone building at the edge of both.

Boulder Experiments in Art and Technology (B.E.A.T.) is a community I started in 2019 for people working at the intersection of art, technology, and science — an open-mic-meets-show-and-tell where anyone can bring a project, finished or in progress, to share.

The group has grown into its own thing: an ongoing series of meetups around Boulder, and in 2021 its first museum show, Convivial Machines, at the Museum of Boulder — an exhibit split into contrasting light and dark halves, built entirely from work by the community.

B.E.A.T. still meets regularly. The next date is always posted on the Meetup page.
